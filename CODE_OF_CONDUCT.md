# Code of conduct

This is a working project for people who build and run construction jobs. Be direct, be kind and assume the
other person knows something you do not.

- Criticise the work, not the person. Disagree with evidence.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or publishing other people's private information.
- Do not post confidential project, client or contract information in issues or pull requests. Redact it.
- Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

Report a problem privately through a [security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) or by contacting
[Constructelligence](https://constructelligence.co). Reports are handled by the maintainers and kept
confidential.
